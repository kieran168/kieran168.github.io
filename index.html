Latest build

How late is your NJ Transit train?

live · python · github actions · public data

NJ Transit publishes two on-time figures for every rail line. One counts every late train. The other removes delays the agency attributes to Amtrak, and that is the one they lead with. This reads both files every week and puts them side by side, for all eight lines, back to 2017.

Counting every late train
85.5%
Amtrak-adjusted
92.1%

Northeast Corridor, the twelve months to June 2026. System-wide over the same period was 89.1%. Every number on the page is recomputed from NJ Transit's own files by a script anyone can run.

About

I've been building things in spreadsheets since I was twenty. The tools got bigger; the instinct didn't. What I care about most is the last mile of a data problem — a dashboard nobody can reach is the same as no dashboard. The work I'm proudest of is rarely the cleverest query. It's the thing that finally put the right numbers in front of the person who needed them, in a format they'd actually open.

The side projects are the same instinct at small scale. I notice a bit of friction in my own week, and instead of tolerating it I go build the smallest thing that removes it. Most of them are a Google Sheet, a script, and a trigger. They're still running, which is the only review that counts.
