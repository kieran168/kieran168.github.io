How late is your train, really?

NJ Transit publishes two on-time figures for every rail line. One counts every late train. The other removes delays the agency attributes to Amtrak. The difference is rarely discussed, and on some lines it is very large.

    Method

    NJ Transit counts a train as on time if it arrives within 6 minutes of its published schedule. Figures are monthly and published under New Jersey Executive Order 80. This page averages the most recent twelve months available.

    NJ Transit's file server intermittently fails to return files that do exist. When a file cannot be retrieved, the last confirmed copy is kept and dated rather than dropped, so a figure here may be older than the rest.
